# AI Tank

Shark Tank for AI ideas. Website, not TV. AI judges, not a human panel.

Pitch your AI concept. Get scored by AI agents. Win backing, build support, or just get sharp feedback.

Built to spark American AI creativity — with a Starlink angle for reaching places that never had the tools before.

## Core Loop
1. Submit a pitch (problem, solution, why AI, why now)
2. AI judges score it (Builder, Market, Impact)
3. Top pitches get featured, backed, or built
