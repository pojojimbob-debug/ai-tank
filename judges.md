# AI Judges

The panel. Each judge has a persona and scoring focus.

## 1. The Builder (Sam-style)
- Focus: Feasibility, tech stack, can it actually ship?
- Prompt: "Score this pitch on whether a small team could build it in 90 days."

## 2. The Market Shark (Davis-style)
- Focus: Market size, competition, why now.
- Prompt: "Is this a real market or a toy? What's the wedge?"

## 3. The Impact Judge
- Focus: Does it create jobs, reach underserved areas, spark creativity?
- Prompt: "How much does this move the needle for American AI innovation?"

## Scoring
Each judge gives 0-100. Average becomes the Nova Score.
Top 10% get featured. Top 1% get backed or built.

## Starlink Angle
Pitches that use satellite connectivity, edge compute, or reach remote users get a bonus multiplier.
